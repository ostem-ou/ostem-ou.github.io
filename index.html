What we do

Three things, done well

Everything we build ladders up to one of these — if it doesn't, it's probably not ours to say.

Belonging

Chosen family first. Y'all get a seat at the table before you have to earn one — no exceptions, no auditions.

Grow

Résumé nights, grad-school panels, internship leads, and a straight shot to the oSTEM National Conference. Career development is the whole point.

Learn

Queer STEM history, role models, and the terminology 101 nobody teaches in lecture. Curiosity pointed at each other, not just our coursework.

Who we are

A chapter of oSTEM Inc, built for OU

We're the local chapter of a national organization for LGBTQ+ folks in science, technology, engineering, and math — and we're built around one idea: the best science happens when no one has to hide.
