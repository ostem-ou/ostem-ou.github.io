About us

The professional community that turns isolation into belonging

For LGBTQ+ students and allies in STEM at OU — because the best science happens when no one has to hide.

Mission

oSTEM @ OU empowers LGBTQ+ students in science, technology, engineering, and math to thrive — personally, academically, and professionally — and builds a community where every Sooner in STEM can bring their whole self to the lab, the lecture hall, and the career fair.

Vision

A University of Oklahoma where being queer in STEM isn't something you navigate around — it's a source of connection, confidence, and creative advantage. Where no one has to choose between their identity and their field.

Our positioning

For LGBTQ+ students and allies in STEM at OU, oSTEM is the professional community that turns isolation into belonging and identity into an advantage — because the best science happens when no one has to hide.

Our national family

Part of oSTEM Inc

oSTEM @ OU is a chapter of Out in Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics (oSTEM Inc), a national nonprofit connecting LGBTQ+ people in STEM across the country. That affiliation means real reach beyond Norman: a network of chapters at other universities, professional partnerships with LGBTQ+-friendly employers, and a seat at the annual oSTEM National Conference — career fair, workshops, and thousands of folks who get it, all in one place.
